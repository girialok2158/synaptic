# 07 — Development Roadmap

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

# Vision

Build **Synaptic** into the world's best AI-powered collaborative learning platform for:

- College Students
- Engineering Students
- Medical Students
- Competitive Exam Aspirants
- Government Exam Aspirants
- Professionals preparing for certifications

The goal is to create a platform where learners can study together, stay motivated, and improve using AI.

---

# Development Philosophy

- Build MVP first
- Ship fast
- Collect feedback
- Improve continuously
- Scale only after validation
- Prioritize quality over quantity
