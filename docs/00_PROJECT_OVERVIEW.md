# 00 — Project Overview

## Project Name

Synaptic

---

## Mission

Synaptic is an AI-powered collaborative learning platform designed to help students preparing for technology careers and competitive examinations learn together, stay productive, and achieve their goals.

---

## Vision

To become the most intelligent and collaborative preparation platform for learners worldwide by combining AI, community learning, productivity tools, and personalized guidance.

---

## Target Users

- Software Engineering Students
- Data Science Aspirants
- AI/ML Students
- UPSC Aspirants
- SSC Aspirants
- Banking Exam Students
- GATE Aspirants
- CAT Aspirants
- Railway Exam Students
- NEET Aspirants
- CLAT Aspirants
- Any learner preparing for structured career goals
