# Synaptic

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# 1. Product Vision

Synaptic is an AI-powered collaborative learning platform that helps students study smarter, remain consistent, and achieve better outcomes through intelligent assistance, peer collaboration, and personalized learning experiences.

Instead of offering isolated tools, Synaptic brings together every essential part of a student's study journey into one seamless ecosystem.

Our objective is to build a platform that students trust every day—not just for AI-generated answers, but for planning, learning, collaborating, revising, and tracking their progress.

---

# 2. Mission

To make high-quality learning accessible, collaborative, personalized, and engaging by combining artificial intelligence with modern productivity tools and peer learning.

# 4. Problem Statement

Today's students use many disconnected platforms.

For example:

• YouTube for lectures

• ChatGPT for doubts

• Google Drive for notes

• Telegram for communities

• WhatsApp for discussions

• Notion for planning

• Anki for flashcards

• Pomodoro apps for focus

Everything is scattered.

This creates friction, distraction, and poor consistency.

Synaptic solves this by providing one unified learning workspace.

# 5. Product Goals

Our goals are to help students:

- Stay consistent
- Collaborate effectively
